Okapi Clip Studio

Okapi Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool used by its operator to produce short vertical clips and publish them to the video channels the operator owns, including the Okapi Gamer and Chilled Clips channels.

What the app does

  • Cuts short clips from long videos, adds captions, and prepares a title and description for each.
  • After the operator approves a clip by hand, uploads it to the operator's own channel, either immediately or on a schedule.
  • Reads view counts and analytics reports for those uploads, so the operator can see which clips perform well.

How Google data is used

The app signs in with Google to get permission to upload videos to the operator's channels and to read their statistics. It does not access any other accounts, and it does not share data with anyone. Details are in the privacy policy.

This page is public and needs no sign-in. The app itself runs only on the operator's computer and is not offered to other users.
