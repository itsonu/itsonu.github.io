Privacy policy: Okapi Clip Studio

Okapi Clip Studio accesses only YouTube channels owned by its operator, using Google sign-in.

  • What it accesses: permission to upload videos to those channels, and read-only access to their video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • How it is used: to publish the operator's own clips and measure how they perform. Nothing else.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Okapi Clip Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
